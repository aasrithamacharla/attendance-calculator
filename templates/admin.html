Salary Calculation & Payment Rules: {{ selected_month }} ({{ working_days_in_month }} Working Days • Sundays Off)

🔒 Salary is finalized once all {{ working_days_in_month }} working days are logged (Sundays excluded)
1. Full Day Employee:

Total leaves = Leaves + (Half / 2) (Sundays excluded).

> 2 leaves: Deduct (Total-2)×Wage/30. < 2: Add (2-Total)×Wage/30.

2. Half Day Employee:

Total leaves = Leaves only (Half days NOT 0.5 leave).

✨ Full day worked = Doubles day salary (+1 day wage bonus)!

3. Part Time Employee:

Half day = 1 day. Full day shifts (Morning, Afternoon, Evening):

Mandatory to select at least 1 shift option. 1 slot = 1 day, 2 = 2 days, 3 = 3 days.
